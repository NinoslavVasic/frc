How this website handles your information

This website belongs to Moonshot Properties LLC, 21-10 51st Avenue, Long Island City, NY 11101. It lists commercial space for lease and lets you send us a requirement.

What we do not do

  • We do not use cookies.
  • We do not use analytics services, advertising pixels, session recording, chat widgets or social media plug-ins.
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising.

What we collect, and only when you choose to send it

If you use the requirement form, we receive what you type: your name, company, email, phone, whether you are a tenant or broker, the space, use, size, move-in date and your message. We use it only to answer you about space. The form is delivered to our leasing inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. To block spam, Web3Forms may check the sender's IP address and email with its spam-filtering providers (CleanTalk and Akismet). If you email or call us, we receive what you send in the usual way.

Services that load as part of the page

  • Hosting. GitHub Pages serves this site. Like any web host, it receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and may keep them in server logs for security.
  • Street map. If you open the street map in the Location section, your browser requests map images from Esri. Esri receives your IP address and the area of the map shown. The map does not load unless you open it.
  • Fonts and map code are served from this site, not from third parties.

Links in our leasing emails

A link in a Moonshot Properties leasing email has a short code at the end of the address. When you open the link, your browser sends that code one time to our campaign tracker, which runs on Google Apps Script. We record that the link was used, and when. This tells us which brokers looked at a space. Google receives your IP address to deliver the request. If you open this site without such a link, nothing is sent. The unsubscribe link works the same way and records your request to be removed.

Links to other sites

Links to LoopNet, Google Maps and other sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. We do not control what they collect.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for as long as we need them to discuss a lease with you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you have sent us, write to admin@moonshot-properties.nyc.
